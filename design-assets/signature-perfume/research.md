# Design review and sources

Reviewed 8 November 2026. This is a focused sample of Bangladesh commerce, not every website.

- Daraz https://www.daraz.com.bd/ - sale/current price hierarchy, category discovery, shopping support.
- Othoba https://othoba.com/ - department directory, hot deals, visible return/support links.
- Shajgoj https://shop.shajgoj.com/ - beauty-specific departments and offers.
- Aarong https://www.aarong.com/bgd - category-first brand organization; page content was partially JavaScript-dependent.
- Pickaboo/Chaldal could not be retrieved with the research tool.

Applied: strong campaign imagery, readable prices, restrained surfaces, clear purchase CTAs, explicit information rather than invented trust claims. Fragrance notes are olfactory descriptors, not ingredient lists. New perfume products, prices, photos and profiles are labeled conceptual samples, editable by merchant; there are no invented reviews, longevity claims or discount rates.
